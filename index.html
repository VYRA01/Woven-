Craft

Three things we refuse to rush

  1. 01

    The grind

    Chuck and brisket, minced on site every morning at a coarse setting. Never pre-packed, never yesterday's. It is the whole reason a burger can taste of beef.

  2. 02

    The ageing

    Steaks rest for a minimum of five weeks before they are allowed near the grill. Argentina, USDA Prime and Japanese A5 — three countries, one standard.

  3. 03

    The fire

    Live charcoal, run hot enough to build a crust in seconds. Real smoke, no gas, no shortcuts. You can watch all of it from the open pass.
